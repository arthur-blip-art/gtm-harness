# Default scoring

Adjust the weights with the user when they know their market better. Keep the reasons in the output.

| Rule | Points |
|---|---|
| Decision role: head of product, technology or partnerships at any size inside the ICP band; founder or CEO up to ~200 people | +3 |
| Influencer role: country lead, business unit lead, head of a relevant product line | +2 |
| Sales, marketing, customer success | +0, useful for an introduction, say so |
| Session on our topic: the abstract is about the problem we solve (for an integration product, connecting systems or moving data between them), whatever side it argues | +3 |
| Session on an adjacent topic (regulation, AI in the same workflow). When in doubt, this one, and say why | +1 |
| Company is `already_targeted` with a documented reason to talk | +3 |
| Company is `target` | +2 |
| Evidence of fit read on the company's own site (the ICP's best predictor, for example a list of integrations) | +2 |
| Sells in several countries we cover | +1 |
| Warm path visible on the event pages or in the user's lists | +2 |
| Findable: named session with date, time and room | +1 |
| Company far above the ICP size band, with a source | -2 |
| Company below the band | -1; under about ten people, bench |
| Size not checked | 0, and write "not checked" |
| Fact not confirmed on re-read | -1 per fact, and drop the fact from the brief |

**Priority.** 1 = target or already targeted company with a decision or influencer role identified on site. 2 = target company with no named person, or only sales and marketing roles. 3 = context: customers, competitors, partners.

**Ties.** Prefer the person whose session comes first: a meeting on day one can lead to a second on day two.

**One per company** in the top 5. When the best-fit company only sends a sales role, keep it in the top 5 if its company score is the highest of the list, and say the meeting is a route to an introduction.
